Realtime Ant Formicarium (400×300)

Nest rules:
  • Ants move 1 pixel per step, only into adjacent empty cells (no jumps).
  • Ants remove only 1 adjacent soil pixel per dig step.
  • Multiple ants may occupy the same pixel.
  • Ants prefer shared tunnel fronts so tunnels and rooms grow cooperatively.
  • Dig direction is angled/randomized (not straight down-only).
  • About 1 in 10 ants self-elects as a room-builder near active tunnels.
  • Surface texture variation is capped to a maximum 3px height difference.
  • After removing 10 soil pixels, an ant returns to the surface, deposits one soil pixel, then walks sideways away from the hole.
